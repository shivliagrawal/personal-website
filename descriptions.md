Q. Why is it called a PowerCell?
A. Previously there was the concept of teams which had a set heirarchy like people managers that managed technical, finance and other aspects. But these methods were archaic, and caused wastage of time and resources. Hence Cummins moved from this "Project Management" style to now the "Product Managament" style. Now the approach is "Product Centric". 

Q. What is PowerSuite?
A. It is a webapp that contains multiple applications within it which are used by Sales Representatives and the clients. Basically both sellers and buyers use it. It contains the following tools - 
- 'GenCalc': calculates range of project parameters
- 'Library': provides product related documents and SOPs 
- 'GenSize': Sizing tool for generators
- 'Power Zone Standalone Configurator': Preview Configuration and download Bills of Material 
- 'CPQ Standalone Configurator': Configures CPQ products without creating a Salesforce account (i.e. an internal pricing tool)
- 'Site Specific Rating (SSR)': Gives an estimate and rating based on geographical location of the requirement for generator installation
- 'GenSpec': Configure and download generator specifications sheet
- 'MarketIntelligence': It is a tool that uses genAI to produce business insights by comparing Cummins products with competitor products (Caterpillar, Kohler, MTU etc) 
- 'GridWizard': Region based product quantity tracker


Each of the above tools are individual projects.
